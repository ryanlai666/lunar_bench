RustLander-v0 model and limitations
=================================
This is a deterministic custom rigid-point landing surrogate implemented in Rust,
NOT a port of Gymnasium LunarLander-v3 or of Box2D. Do not compare its reward
numbers or solved threshold directly with Gymnasium. No Box2D fidelity is claimed.

Observations: x, y above flat pad, vx, vy, angle, angular velocity, two leg flags.
Actions: 0 coast, 1 positive rotation torque, 2 main thrust, 3 negative torque.
Initial x is uniform [-.25,.25], height 1.2; random vx [-.15,.15], vy/angle/omega
[-.1,.1]. SplitMix64 generates the initial state; subsequent transitions are
fully deterministic. Units are normalized model units, not Gymnasium units.

Integration: semi-implicit Euler, dt=.02; gravity=-1.5; main thrust=3.2;
side torque=+/-4; lateral side acceleration=-/+0.08; linear damping=.999 per
step and angular damping=.98. No terrain, wind, articulated legs, collision
solver, contact impulses, engine dispersion, fuel limits or Box2D sleep state.

Touchdown at y<=0 succeeds iff abs(x)<=.20, abs(vx)<=.25, abs(vy)<=.30,
abs(angle)<=.20 and abs(omega)<=.40. Both leg flags then turn on. Touchdown
terminates immediately; there is no simulated settling period. Other touchdown
is a crash. Leaving abs(x)>1, y>2.5 or abs(angle)>pi also terminates as failure.
A time limit (default 1000 steps) truncates and is not a success or crash.

Reward is the difference in potential (-100*distance to origin -100*speed
-100*abs(angle) +10*each leg contact), minus .3 main-engine or .03 side-engine
cost. Terminal reward REPLACES the shaping reward with +100 success or -100
failure. Potential is initialized on reset. Reward is not clipped or normalized.

Independent native batch environments use separate deterministic state. Batch
steps reject all actions atomically if any action is invalid or any member is
already ended. Python requires explicit resets and validates shapes and types.
C ABI ownership: rl_create/rl_destroy; rl_reset writes 12 floats; rl_step writes
count*12 floats. Buffers and handles must be valid, nonoverlapping and owned by
the caller; C ABI is not safe against arbitrary invalid pointers.

Build from repository root using lunar_bench/build_native.ps1. Native tests cover
replay, seed variation, action validation, termination/truncation, landing/crash,
thrust direction and finite rollouts. benchmark_native.py writes real scalar,
batched FFI and in-Rust throughput with transitions and elapsed seconds.
