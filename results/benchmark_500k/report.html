Learning curves

How to read this comparison

  • Test metrics evaluate each final fixed-budget checkpoint deterministically on held-out environment seeds starting at 2,000,000,000. Validation seeds start at 1,000,000,000 and are not test seeds.
  • Mean return gives equal weight to each training seed. The 95% interval bootstraps independent training-seed means with 20,000 draws. With only three training seeds, uncertainty is poorly resolved; 300 test episodes are not 300 independent training replicates. Intervals do not capture all environment-seed uncertainty.
  • Landing fraction uses explicit success in the Rust simulator, or Gymnasium true termination with final reward at least 99. It is distinct from the fraction of episodes scoring at least 200, and time-limit truncation is not a landing.
  • Training seconds include rollout simulation and optimizer updates, and exclude validation, final evaluation and file-writing outside the timed training block. Transitions/s equals training transitions divided by those seconds. Run wall seconds include evaluation and checkpoint/report work after environment initialization.
  • All learners use NumPy on CPU. Rust accelerates the custom simulator only. The Rust model differs from Box2D in physical dynamics, contact handling and task difficulty; this is not an equivalent-engine speed comparison.
  • Methods receive equal transition budgets, not equal optimizer steps or compute. PPO/A2C use one 64-unit tanh hidden layer and rewards scaled by 0.01 for optimization; Double DQN uses two 64-unit ReLU layers and unscaled rewards. Reported rewards are always raw. The comparison concerns these implementations and settings, not algorithms in isolation.
  • Hyperparameters were fixed for these runs. A finite budget does not establish convergence or a universal winner. Six concurrent processes contend for CPU resources; per-run times are measured under this workload. Any optional matched serial/parallel measurement is a separate fixed-workload pair; per-run contended timings alone are not a serial speedup estimate.
